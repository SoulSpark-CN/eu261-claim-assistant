British Airways flight disrupted? Claim EUR 250–600

British Airways is a UK carrier, so its flights departing the EU, and those it operates arriving in the EU, are covered by EU261. BA's New York → London flights ARE covered.

Amounts

≤1500km €250 · 1500-3500km €400 · >3500km €600. Triggers: arrival delay ≥3h, cancellation within 14 days, denied boarding.

Claim it

Submit to BA's claims portal with your booking + arrival time. BA sometimes rejects with "extraordinary circumstances" — push back with proof. The UK CAA is the free escalation route (6-year limit in the UK).
