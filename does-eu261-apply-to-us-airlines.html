Do American, Delta & United flights to Europe qualify?

Short answer: usually NOT for the US→Europe leg, but YES for the Europe→US return.

The rule

EU261 covers flights departing an EU airport (any airline) or arriving at an EU airport on an EU/EEA airline. American, Delta and United are NOT EU airlines.

So:

  • United New York → London → NOT covered
  • United London → New York → covered (departs EU)

What to do

Check your flight's direction + operator. If it's NOT covered, there's no EU261 claim — but you may still have other rights (check your ticket terms / the airline's policy).
