Cancelled within 14 days — you're likely owed €250–€600

Under EU261, a cancellation qualifies for compensation if the airline told you 14 days or fewer before departure. Told you earlier (and rerouted)? Usually not.

Amount

€250 / €400 / €600 by distance. Same tiers as delays.

Also: a refund or rerouting is separate

Even if you're not owed compensation, a cancelled flight can entitle you to a refund or rerouting plus meals/hotel. Don't confuse the two.
