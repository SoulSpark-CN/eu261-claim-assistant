Flight delayed in Europe? What you're owed (2026)

Three triggers

  • Arrival delay ≥3h
  • Cancellation notified within 14 days
  • Denied boarding due to overbooking

Amounts (per person)

≤1500km €250 · 1500-3500km €400 · >3500km €600

Who's covered

Departures from an EU airport (any airline), or EU-arrival on an EU/EEA airline. A United New York→London flight is NOT covered; a British Airways one IS.

Exemptions & time

Weather/ATC/strikes can be argued as extraordinary circumstances. Time limits: UK 6 yrs, Germany 3, France 5, Netherlands 2.
