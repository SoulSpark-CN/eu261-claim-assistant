How to claim EU261 compensation from a US airline

First, check eligibility — it decides everything. A United / Delta / American flight from New York or LA to London/Paris/Frankfurt is NOT covered (US departure + US airline). Don't waste weeks claiming for a flight the regulation doesn't protect.

Steps

  1. Evidence: boarding pass, actual arrival time, disruption notice, booking reference.
  2. Write the claim: to the airline's official claims portal. Reference Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 and the amount (€250/€400/€600 by distance).
  3. Expect a fight: airlines say "weather/ATC" — ask them to prove it.
  4. Escalate free: National Enforcement Body (UK CAA, German Luftfahrt-Bundesamt…). Airlines often settle.
  5. Small claims: EU small-claims courts handle up to €2,000, simplified and cheap.

Don't lose your right

Time limits: UK 6 yrs, Germany 3, France 5, Netherlands 2. Check your country.
