Is your US airline flight to Europe covered by EU261?

The single most costly misunderstanding: refunds. EU261 covers more flights than Americans think — but also fewer. It depends on the departure airport and the airline.

The two rules

  1. Departs from an EU airport → covered (any airline). e.g. a United flight London→New York IS covered.
  2. Arrives at an EU airport AND operated by an EU/EEA airline → covered. e.g. Lufthansa New York→Frankfurt IS covered.

The common trap

  • ✈️ United, New York → London → NOT covered
  • ✈️ Lufthansa, New York → Frankfurt → Covered
  • ✈️ British Airways, New York → London → Covered

What you're owed

≤1500km €250 · 1500-3500km €400 · >3500km €600. Triggered by arrival delay ≥3h, cancellation within 14 days, or denied boarding (overbooking).

When they can refuse

Weather, air-traffic control, and strikes are "extraordinary circumstances." Technical faults and overbooking are not.
