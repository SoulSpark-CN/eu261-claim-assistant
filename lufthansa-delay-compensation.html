Lufthansa delay or cancellation — you could be owed up to EUR 600

Lufthansa is an EU/EEA carrier, so its flights arriving in the EU (e.g. New York → Frankfurt) AND all flights departing the EU are covered by EU261.

What you're owed

≤1500km €250 · 1500-3500km €400 · >3500km €600 (transatlantic = €600). Triggered by arrival delay ≥3h, cancellation within 14 days, or denial boarding.

The twist

Because Lufthansa is an EU carrier, even its US→Europe flights (where most American carriers are NOT covered) ARE covered. This trips up a LOT of travelers.

How to claim

Use the checker below, then submit a written claim to Lufthansa's official channel. If they say "weather/ATC", ask for proof. Escalate free to the German Luftfahrt-Bundesamt if needed.
